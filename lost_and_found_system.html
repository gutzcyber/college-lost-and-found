How The System Works

1. Report

Submit a detailed report of the item you lost or found on campus.

2. Search

Browse the listings using filters to find matching items.

3. Claim

If you see your item, submit a secure claim with proof of ownership.

4. Verify & Collect

Admin verifies the claim and arranges for the safe return of the item.

System Rules & Guidelines

  • Do not submit false reports or spam the system.
  • Do not share sensitive personal information publicly in item descriptions.
  • Only claim items that genuinely belong to you. False claims may result in disciplinary action.
  • All high-value items must be handed over to the central Lost & Found office for verification.
  • Items unclaimed after 90 days may be donated or discarded per college policy.
